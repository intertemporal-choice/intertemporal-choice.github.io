# The Book as a PDF

The whole book except the Supplemental Notes is available as a single PDF: [intertemporal-choice.pdf](https://intertemporal-choice.github.io/intertemporal-choice.pdf).

The PDF is made from the same source as these pages, but only when the authors mark a release, so it can lag behind the website by whatever has changed since. Before a release is published, its PDF is built from scratch and checked: it must have no LaTeX errors, broken cross-references, missing characters or lines running into the margin. Earlier releases are kept on the book's [releases page](https://github.com/intertemporal-choice/intertemporal-choice.github.io/releases).

The PDF leaves out the notebooks and the Supplemental Notes; its links to them, and to the Math Facts, open them online.
