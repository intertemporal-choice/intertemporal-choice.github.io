# Mathematical Appendices

We use the results collected here repeatedly in the main chapters, but deriving them
where they are first needed would interrupt the argument.

They cover the conditions under which the behavior of many heterogeneous households
collapses into a single aggregate relationship, a side-by-side comparison of the discrete
and continuous time formulations of the Ramsey problem, and a list of the mathematical
facts this literature leans on most often (geometric sums and logarithmic
approximations, mostly).
